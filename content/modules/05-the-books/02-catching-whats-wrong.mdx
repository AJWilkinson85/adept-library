## The bookkeeper's question

A good bookkeeper does not just categorize. They notice. The vendor that charged twice. The invoice you sent that never got paid. The software subscription nobody has logged into since spring. The model is good at this because it is pattern-matching, which is exactly what noticing is.

## The recipe

<Prompt title="Review a month for problems">
Here is my business brief: [paste]

Below are last month's transactions (already categorized) and, separately, the invoices I sent in the last 90 days with their status. Review them like a careful bookkeeper and list, in order of importance:
1. Possible duplicate charges (same vendor, same or similar amount, within a few days).
2. Recurring charges: every subscription or monthly vendor, with amount, and flag any I should confirm I still use.
3. Invoices I sent that are past due, with age and amount.
4. Anything that looks unusual for a business like mine: an amount far larger than that vendor's usual, a vendor I've never used, a charge on a weekend that looks personal.
5. A three-sentence plain-English summary of the month: what came in, what went out, and the one thing I should look at first.

Be specific. Reference the transaction by date and description. If you are not sure something is a problem, say so and say why.

## What it is not

This is not an audit, and it is not a substitute for your accountant. The tool will occasionally flag a normal charge as unusual and occasionally miss something. It is a second look, done every month, that costs five minutes. The value is in the consistency: problems get caught in the month they happen instead of at tax time.

## Turning it into a monthly routine

Run the categorization recipe, then this one, on the first business day of the month. Twenty minutes total. Put the summary paragraph at the top of a running document called "Monthly notes" so you can see the year in twelve paragraphs.

<Worksheet title="Review last month">
- Run the review recipe on the month you categorized in lesson 1.
- Go through the recurring-charges list and cancel anything you actually do not use. Write down what that saves per year.
- Chase the past-due invoices with the follow-up recipe from Module 3.
- Start the "Monthly notes" document with this month's summary paragraph.
- Add the recipe to the library with CHECK: "Confirm a flagged duplicate against the bank before disputing."
</Worksheet>
